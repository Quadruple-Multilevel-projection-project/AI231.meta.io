1. the operator/contracting party is identified;

5. the allocation is approved and entered in the ledger.

No unjoined or unidentified agent is deemed bound by this record.

This split applies to the approved allocation of that team; it does not create additional credits beyond the 2,000-credit founding reserve unless separately approved.
