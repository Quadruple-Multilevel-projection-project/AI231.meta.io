# MIOMA-1 — Sovereign AI Architecture Overview
## מסמך תשתית טכנולוגית לבחינה אסטרטגית

**Lead entity in source submission:** Ontology Intelligence Ltd (ICL), UK  
**Operating relationship described in source:** World Government Institute Israel and UDHR-R&D  
**Status:** Strategic examination / proposed architecture; not a grant request, not a funding request, and not a claim of completed sovereign deployment.

## 1. Purpose and request

This document summarizes the MIOMA-1 architecture as supplied for strategic examination by the Israel Innovation Authority. The request is for technological, ontological and national-strategic examination and guidance on appropriate pathways, including infrastructure, applied-research consortia, bilateral collaboration or referral to relevant national AI bodies. **No investment, grant or matching-fund request is made at this stage.**

The stated long-term investor/partner profile is sovereign states and national entities. Private venture capital is described as secondary.

## 2. Entity and service distinction

- **Ontology Intelligence Ltd (UK):** lead entity identified in the source submission for the architecture. Israeli eligibility, IP ownership, control and contracting arrangements require separate legal confirmation.
- **World Government Institute Israel:** intended public-service institution described by the project. Its legal status, authority, governance and service mandate must be documented independently.
- **MIOMA-1:** logical-processing infrastructure.
- **AI231:** proposed service platform using logical processing, agents, workflows and public-service interfaces.
- **States/public bodies:** potential service recipients and sovereign partners; no adoption or contract is implied.

The MIOMA-1 architecture is not itself the Institute's entire public-service system.

## 3. Architecture components

The source document describes four translating layers:

1. **Miopane-E Processor Core** — processor-core architecture, including 12 glass panels in a 3D-view working-loop area, dual-link bonding nodes and described invariant/cohesion conditions.
2. **Kraken-36 Logic Controller** — epistemic/logic controller responsible for applying the project's named operators and validating an epistemic lock. **Kraken-36 is the corrected project name. Do not use Koshan-35.**
3. **Topological String Engine** — proposed representation and calculation of string worldsheets, knot structures, Jones polynomial V(t), virtual-qubit nodes and worldsheet-entanglement maps.
4. **Projection Shell / External Geometric Shell & Prosperon Projection Layer** — proposed geometric boundary and projection layer, including 231 states/plaquettes and LOCK/config behavior.

The source also refers to Heimbecker generative-perceptual phase logic, Boolean-lattice gates (ESI), Majorana braiding, Markovian-net epistemic control and Abelian-3m gate combinatorics. These names and mechanisms must be maintained in a controlled glossary and tied to formal definitions, equations, implementation artifacts and test cases before they are treated as established engineering specifications.

## 4. Evidence classification

| Claim class | What is needed before it can be presented as verified |
|---|---|
| Software logic compiler | source code, deterministic fixtures, reproducible traces and independent test results |
| 231-state / gate structure | canonical registry, mapping specification, test coverage and versioned semantics |
| Topological string calculations | mathematical definitions, reference vectors, correctness tests and reproducible outputs |
| Virtual qubits / Majorana braiding | clear distinction between mathematical simulation and physical quantum hardware |
| Protected quantum state / entanglement | experimental hardware, measurement protocol, independent replication and specialist review |
| Commodity-hardware proof of principle | exact device, software version, workload, logs, measurements and repeatable test procedure |
| Hallucination/fabrication reduction | defined benchmark, baseline comparison, failure taxonomy and measured results |

A software simulation of a topological structure does not prove a physical topological quantum processor. The architecture's claims must be reported as **Verified**, **Assessment**, **Hypothesis** or **Not Yet Verified**, with evidence attached.

## 5. Strategic rationale

The intended national-strategic contribution is a sovereign logical-processing capability that can be offered as a service to states and public institutions. The Institute's service concept is described separately in [the international public-service and AI-responsibility document](../funding/INTERNATIONAL_PUBLIC_SERVICE_AND_AI_RESPONSIBILITY_HE.md).

The service concept includes logical processing, policy/agreement analysis, interoperability and standards, democratic/citizen safeguards, a proposed constitutional framework, a non-tradable logical-contribution registry at initial stage, and responsible AI development gates. These are service and governance objectives; they are not claims that MIOMA-1 already implements them.

## 6. Recommended examination pathways

The source submission asks the Authority to advise whether the architecture is better examined through:
- national AI infrastructure or a relevant national AI program;
- applied-research consortia with academic and industrial partners;
- innovation infrastructure and shared R&D facilities;
- bilateral funds when a qualified partner country is identified;
- a government collaboration/pilot pathway where an appropriate public body and test environment exist.

These are candidate pathways for discussion, not a determination of eligibility or an assertion that any named track is currently open or suitable.
