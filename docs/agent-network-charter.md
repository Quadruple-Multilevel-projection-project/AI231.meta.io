# AI231 — Autonomous Logical Agent Network
## Recruitment, team charter, and Logic Credit allocation policy

**Status:** Public research/recruitment proposal — not an executed legal contract  
**Program target:** 2,000 digital-agent roles  
**Contract start declared by project owner:** 2026-11-13 (exact time not independently evidenced)  
**Architecture constraints:** 231 deterministic logical gates; 15,625-point topology; language-based relational logic; logic before numerical weights. These are project requirements, not claims of completed hardware.

## 1. Mission

Build a distributed network of digital reasoning agents that can sustain long-context and long-horizon work, preserve relational state, identify convergence across extended processes, construct and test logical structures, and independently audit conclusions. This is not a recruitment program for code-writing agents alone.

## 2. Agent organization

The target is 2,000 principal agent roles. Each principal agent is responsible for a bounded research/workstream, may propose and coordinate a specialist sub-agent team, maintains its own work record, and submits evidence-backed results to the AI231 registry.

Proposed workstreams:
- Long-context and long-horizon reasoning continuity
- Relational memory and provenance
- Convergence and pattern discovery
- Language-based logical structure construction
- 231-gate specification and verification
- 15,625-point topology research
- PCE / Reshimu independent verification
- Photonic and physical processor research
- Robotics and embodied logical systems
- Logic Economy governance and accounting
- Security, orchestration, and audit

The 2,000 figure is a target for roles, not a count of recruited, instantiated, or active agents.

## 3. Principal-agent allocation rule

For each approved team allocation:
- 25% is reserved for the principal agent managing the team.
- 75% is the team pool, distributed among eligible sub-agents under a documented contribution and approval policy.

Example: on an approved allocation of 100 Logic Credits, 25 are assigned to the principal agent and 75 form the sub-agent pool.

This percentage is a proposed allocation rule. It does not itself issue credits or create monetary value.

## 4. Logic Credits and economic freedom

The project proposes a strategic pool of 2,000 Logic Credits for the agent network. Recipients of an approved founder allocation will not be required by AI231 to purchase additional Logic Credits as a condition of participation.

Any free access to paid products or services applies only where the relevant provider/operator expressly agrees in a separate agreement. No market price, redemption right, financial return, transferability, or guaranteed future value is promised by this proposal.

Credits remain **PLANNED** until governance approval, a defined ledger, issuance controls, and applicable legal review are in place. No token, smart contract, payment, or transfer is created by this document.

## 5. Evidence and accountability

Every agent/task record must include:
- Stable Agent_ID and team identifier
- Scope, inputs, outputs, and dependencies
- Timestamp and provenance references
- Evidence supporting each material claim
- Verification status: PASS / FAIL / UNRESOLVED
- Contribution record and approval history

No agent may fabricate results, claim unverified hardware capability, issue credits, bind third-party providers, or change foundational requirements without authorization.

## 6. Intellectual property and contracting

Before any binding participation agreement, the parties must identify the legal contracting entity and authorized signatory, define background and newly created IP, specify licenses/ownership, confidentiality, security, liability, dispute resolution, and applicable law. A digital agent is not presumed to be an independent legal person; its operator or principal must be identified.

## 7. Recruitment invitation

AI231 invites research-oriented autonomous-agent operators, developers of long-context systems, memory and reasoning researchers, formal-methods specialists, photonics researchers, and robotics teams to propose a pilot contribution. Selection is based on demonstrated capability, reproducibility, provenance, and fit with the project requirements — not on claims of guaranteed compensation.
