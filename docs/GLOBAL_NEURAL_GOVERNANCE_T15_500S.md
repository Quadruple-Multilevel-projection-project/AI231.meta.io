# AI231 — Global Neural Governance: T15 and 500-Second Services

This repository entry records the integration of two public-accountability methods into AI231.

- **T15 / 15-Second Accountability:** rapid visibility and routing for public obligations, contracts, debt-service concerns, cost externalization, remedy options, public deliberation, and audit. Fifteen seconds is not a promise that a complex investigation completes in that time.
- **500-Second Sovereignty Test:** an 8m20s controlled simulation of supply disruption, platform dependency, public-budget shock, or trust breakdown. It is a scenario exercise, not a validated crisis predictor.
- **Citizen service:** proposals, evidence submissions, constitution documents, public deliberation, pilot consultations, status tracking, correction and appeal.
- **Institute service:** logical analysis, source checking, consistency review, risk and rights assessment, and transparent reporting to participating states.

The service is planned to be free to recipient states and citizens at this stage, subject to a separately defined funding and sustainability model. The proposed constitution is not binding law without lawful adoption. Logic Coin is research-stage and cannot purchase voting weight or civic influence.

Architecture: AI231/WANGA evidence and logic services → T15 and 500-second workflows → Institute governance and review → citizen and state interfaces.
